Rules & FAQ

How It Works

  • Pick one NFL team each week that you believe will win.
  • If your team wins, you advance.
  • If your team loses or ties, you are eliminated unless your Week 1–4 strike saves you.
  • You can use each NFL team only once during the season.

Entry & Prize

  • One entry per person.
  • $20 entry fee.
  • Winner takes all.

Strikes

Each player gets one strike through Week 4. If you lose while the strike is available, you remain alive but your strike is consumed and the losing team cannot be used again. After Week 4, there are no more strikes.

Commissioner

Matt is the commissioner. In the event of an unusual circumstance, the commissioner may resolve a technical or administrative issue to keep the pool fair.

Commissioner Dashboard

This prototype does not yet have secure commissioner authentication. Do not publish it as a live league until the Google Apps Script backend is connected.
